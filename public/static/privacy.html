Privacy policy

This policy is a template. The operator of this site replaces the bracketed details before publishing.

Slotwise helps organizers share availability and lets invitees book, reschedule, or cancel meetings. This site is operated by [Operator name] ("we") at [site address]. This policy covers this Slotwise site only.

Account and booking data

We store organizer account details, schedules, and booking settings. When you book, we store your name, email, meeting time, time zone, and any phone number, guest email addresses, or answers you submit. The organizer can see this information to manage your meeting. Anyone with a public booking link can see the organizer's published booking details and available times.

Google Calendar access

Connecting Google Calendar is optional for organizers. Slotwise uses the Google account email to identify the connection and lists calendar names so the organizer can select calendars to check for conflicts and a calendar for new bookings. It uses free and busy times to calculate availability. When you reschedule a booking, Slotwise reads event IDs, times, status, availability, and attendee response statuses. It uses these fields to check availability without counting the original booking or events that the calendar's own attendee declined.

For calendar-backed bookings, Slotwise sends meeting details to the selected Google calendar, including attendee names and email addresses, meeting time, and submitted booking information such as phone number and answers. Google Calendar can send invitations and updates to attendees. Depending on the calendar and event's visibility settings, people with access to the event may also see its details. Slotwise can request a Google Meet link when the organizer chooses Google Meet, and update or delete the event when the booking is moved or canceled.

Storage and sharing

Booking and account records are stored in the database of this site: [hosting provider and database, for example Cloudflare D1 or a SQLite file on our own server]. Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest and used on the server to keep the organizer's connection working. Booking records can include the calendar and event identifiers and meeting link for a booking. The service records scheduling notifications in an internal outbox. Outgoing email: [email provider, or "no email provider: notifications are only logged"]. Google Calendar may separately send event messages.

Slotwise uses Google data to provide Calendar and booking features. We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or provide it to data brokers. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deleting data

Organizers can disconnect a Google account in Integrations to remove its stored connection and tokens, and can revoke access in their Google Account. Disconnecting does not remove existing meetings from Google Calendar. Canceling a booking changes its status and does not erase the booking record.

For privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of your data, contact [Operator name] at [contact email].
